ABOUT US

Smart Ambulance System is an AWS cloud-based solution designed to provide fast, reliable and efficient emergency ambulance services. Our goal is to reduce emergency response time by connecting patients, ambulances and hospitals through a smart digital platform. The system helps users quickly request an ambulance, allows hospitals to receive patient information in advance, and improves overall emergency management. Using AWS Cloud Services, the platform is scalable, secure and capable of supporting future features like live tracking, automated alerts and real-time monitoring.
